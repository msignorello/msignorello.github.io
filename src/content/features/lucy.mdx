Lucy is a French bulldog, unofficial morale officer, and a reliable reminder that every intense workday benefits from a walk, a snack, or an aggressively scheduled nap.

She has held several informal leadership roles over the years, including branch inspector, neighborhood watch coordinator, and chief squeaker examiner. Her management style is visible, direct, and highly food motivated.
