TechBoot is a technology services and business consulting firm based in Northern New Jersey. I started it near the end of 2011 because I wanted to combine hands-on technical experience with the operating lessons that come from working inside larger corporate organizations.

The useful work usually lives between strategy and execution. A business may know something is not working, but still need help diagnosing the real problem, choosing the right tradeoffs, and getting the plan into motion.

## Areas where I can help

- Business technology strategy
- Hardware and infrastructure sourcing
- Product management and systems design
- Technical operations and support workflows
- User growth, activation, and revenue experiments

## The operating bias

I prefer practical progress over theater. That usually means starting with the business problem, understanding the people and constraints around it, and then choosing a technical path that can be supported after the initial project is done.

The best engagements are clear, measurable, and grounded in what the team can actually operate.
